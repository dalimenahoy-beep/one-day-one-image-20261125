**The World Gazette** — all of Wednesday 25 November 2026 on one front page.

The lead: a Brooklyn jury convicts Huawei on 10 of 12 counts. Around it:
Zelenskiy confirms the strike on a Russian ship in the Gulf of Finland, the
AI trade reverses as Nvidia slides, Houthis hit Riyadh's tallest tower and

Sarkozy is sent to trial, a Pakistani pilot becomes Tiangong's first foreign
astronaut, 25 hostages in a Cartagena supermarket, Malaysia's plan to return
6,500 Rohingya, the CIA/ODNI cyber breach, AIDS deaths at a 20-year low, and
a 5.6 earthquake in Nepal — plus a Day in Numbers box and a wire ticker.

## How it works

- `make_poster.py` draws the poster at build time with Python/PIL from the
  day's researched, sourced stories and writes `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG).
- `entry.png` is committed; `main.ts` (Node standard library only) copies it
  byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when the grader runs it.
